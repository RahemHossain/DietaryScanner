## Overview

**Dietary Scanner** is an Android application designed to help users identify and track food products that meet their specific dietary requirements. Born out of necessity and developed by students, this app provides a convenient way to scan, save, and manage food products based on dietary preferences like Halal, Kosher, Vegan, and Vegetarian options.

## Features

### 🔍 Product Scanning & Recognition
- **Camera Integration**: Take photos of food products and ingredients
- **Ingredient Analysis**: Automatically reads and analyzes ingredient lists
- **Product Identification**: Smart recognition of popular food items

### 🥗 Dietary Preference Management
- **Multiple Dietary Options**: Support for Halal, Kosher, Vegan, and Vegetarian diets
- **Custom Dietary Restrictions**: Add custom dietary requirements
- **Preference Filtering**: Filter products based on your selected dietary needs
- **Toggle Controls**: Easy on/off switches for each dietary preference

### 📱 Smart Product Management
- **Saved Products List**: Keep track of products you've scanned
- **Color-Coded Status**: 
  - 🟢 Green: Product meets your dietary requirements
  - 🟡 Yellow: Caution - review ingredients carefully
  - 🔴 Red: Product does not meet your dietary requirements
- **Product Details**: View detailed ingredient information
- **Remove from List**: Easy management of saved items

### 🏠 Browse & Discover
- **Featured Products**: Discover popular and recommended items
- **Category Navigation**: Browse by product categories (Snacks, Medicine, Breakfast, Drinks)
- **Popular Items**: See trending products in the community
- **Search Functionality**: Find specific products quickly

### 👤 User Management
- **Simple & Easy Mode**: Use the app without creating an account, and go directly to the usage you are really after!
- **User Preferences**: Personalized settings and dietary profiles

### ⚙️ Additional Features
- **Privacy Controls**: Transparent privacy settings
- **Donations**: Support charities
- **Rating System**: Rate and review the app
- **Account Management**: Full control over your account and data. (None of your data is saved under the cloud!)

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Android device running Android 6.0 (API level 23) or higher
- Camera permissions for product scanning
- Internet connection for product database access

### Installation

1. **Download the APK** (when available)
   ```bash
   # Download from Google Play Store (coming soon)
   # or sideload the APK file
   ```

2. **Install the Application**
   - Enable "Unknown Sources" in your Android settings if sideloading
   - Install the APK file
   - Grant necessary permissions (Camera, Internet)

3. **First Launch Setup**
   - Choose to sign in or continue as guest
   - Set your dietary preferences
   - Grant camera permissions for scanning
   - Accept terms and conditions

## How to Use

### Setting Up Your Dietary Preferences
1. Open the app and navigate to preferences
2. Toggle on your dietary requirements (Halal, Kosher, Vegan, Vegetarian, +Custom)
3. Add any custom dietary restrictions
4. Save your preferences

### Scanning Products
1. Tap the camera/scan button
2. Point your camera at the product's ingredient list
3. Take a clear photo of the ingredients
4. Wait for the app to analyze the ingredients
5. Review the results and dietary compatibility

### Managing Your Saved Products
1. Access your saved products from the main menu
2. View color-coded compatibility status
3. Tap on products for detailed information
4. Remove products you no longer need

## Technical Details

### Built With
- **Platform**: Android (Native)
- **Minimum SDK**: API 23 (Android 6.0)
- **Target SDK**: API 34 (Android 14)
- **Authentication**: Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In
- **Image Processing**: Camera API integration
- **Database**: Cloud-based product database

### Permissions Required
- `CAMERA` - For scanning product ingredients
- `INTERNET` - For accessing product database
- `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` - For accessing saved images
- `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` - For saving scan results

## Privacy & Security

Dietary Scanner takes user privacy seriously:
- **Data Encryption**: All user data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Minimal Data Collection**: Only necessary information is collected
- **Transparent Privacy Policy**: Clear explanation of data usage
- **User Control**: Users can delete their account and data at any time
- **No Tracking**: No unnecessary user tracking or analytics
